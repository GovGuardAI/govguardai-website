The market gap

Modern oversight tools that fit agency reality.

Many public agencies still rely on manual review, legacy reporting, fragmented systems, and reactive audits to identify irregularities.

GovGuard AI is designed as an intelligent layer above existing systems — not a replacement for the financial, payroll, procurement, or ERP platforms agencies already use.

How it works

A specialized Public-Sector Anomaly Intelligence Model.

01

Ingest ordinary exports

Start with AP records, vendor files, payroll summaries, procurement data, ledger reports, grant disbursements, or restricted-fund records.

02

Normalize and score

Clean records, align vendors and payees, identify unusual patterns, and assign risk scores using domain-specific anomaly logic.

03

Explain and review

Generate ranked findings with reason codes, supporting evidence, confidence levels, comparable context, and recommended review paths.

Initial use cases

Fiscal-risk patterns public agencies can act on.

Accounts payable integrity

Duplicate invoices, duplicate payments, abnormal timing, reimbursement irregularities, and payment outliers.

Vendor risk review

Vendor concentration, recurring patterns, shared attributes, unusual relationships, and network-based risk signals.

Procurement oversight

Threshold avoidance, split purchases, suspicious timing, vendor rotation anomalies, and irregular purchase-order patterns.

Payroll, grants, and funds

Overtime spikes, unusual pay types, grant timing issues, allocation anomalies, restricted-use concerns, and department-level shifts.
